Honeybees can navigate using the Earth’s magnetic field because they have 
microscopic iron oxide particles in their abdomens that act like built-in compasses. 
This incredible biological GPS allows them to find their way across miles of open terrain 
and return to their hives with pinpoint accuracy. Additionally, bees use a highly complex 
"waggle dance" to communicate the precise distance and direction of food sources to their hive 
mates relative to the position of the sun. This combination of internal magnetic sensing and astronomical 
tracking makes them some of the most sophisticated navigators in the entire animal kingdom.
